The Canada Revenue Agency (CRA), a key government body responsible for tax collection and benefit delivery, is committed to fostering a reputation as an inclusive and diverse workplace. To continue to work towards this, the CRA and Sandbox created a video series designed to attract top talent across various fields, including technology, business intelligence, finance, accounting, and data science. Through a series of short live-action videos, the agency sought to highlight its values, celebrate diversity, and position itself as one of Canada’s leading employers, appealing to professionals at different career stages.

In collaboration with the CRA, we produced four impactful live-action videos tailored to specific target audiences, including recent graduates, mid-career professionals, and members of equity-deserving groups. The videos emphasized the CRA’s commitment to diversity, equity, and service excellence, leveraging compelling visual storytelling and multilingual content in both English and Canadian French. By blending narrative framing with cultural sensitivity, we showcased the CRA’s inclusive work culture for their target audience. In order to enable the agency to repurpose content for future promotional activities, we delivered comprehensive source files, ensuring sustained value and usability.

This project successfully enhanced the CRA’s brand as an employer of choice in Canada. By strategically highlighting its inclusive values and opportunities across diverse career paths, the videos resonated with a wide range of audiences, fostering stronger connections with potential candidates. The initiative supported the CRA's recruitment goals, and reinforced its reputation as a modern, forward-thinking organization committed to equity and service excellence, among Canada’s Top 100 Employers.
