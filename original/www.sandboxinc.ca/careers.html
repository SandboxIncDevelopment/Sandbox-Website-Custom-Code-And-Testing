CULTURE

Our workplace is built around people and collaboration—one of our core values. We have ambition but we have fun, we take pride but we remain humble. We support one another in doing great work at a high standard, because we know our work impacts other people’s lives.

We celebrate wins and we take stumbles in stride.

We believe that a high-impact team requires hard skills and an openness to adaptability and learning. We look for creative problem-solvers, whether you have a strong track record in a specific field or you’re still growing into your potential. We aim to be a space where all of us can create, learn and thrive.

PERKS AND
BENEFITS

Thriving Together

Morning Kickoffs: Begin each day with productive team sync-ups to align on goals and tackle challenges.

Collaboration: Participate in regular in-person and virtual meetings that drive teamwork and creativity.

Balanced Work Life

Hybrid Style: Enjoy a balanced work environment with a mix of office and remote days for optimal productivity.

Flexible Hours: Benefit from flexible working hours to suit your personal and professional needs.

Growing with Us

Professional Growth: Thrive in a work culture that encourages you to challenge yourself and grow your skills.

Career Pathways: Benefit from a continuous focus on your professional development and ongoing coaching to boost your career trajectory.

Well-being and Connection

Health First: Access comprehensive health benefits to keep you at your best.

Time Off: Enjoy 20 days of paid time off annually, including a mix of vacation and a company-wide shutdown during the winter holidays.

Celebrations and Fun: Join in regular team events, from festive parties to annual workshops and weekly catch-ups, to build camaraderie.

Inspiring Spaces: Work in renovated loft-style offices designed to spark creativity and focus.
