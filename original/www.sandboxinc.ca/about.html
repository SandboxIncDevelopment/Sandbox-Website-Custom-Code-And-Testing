OUR
HISTORY

2014

Sandbox began with a passion for telling important stories through film, putting its creativity at the service of mission-driven organizations in the public and nonprofit sectors.

2017

Sandbox expands internationally and explores emerging technologies—venturing into VR, interactive formats, and new media—while deepening its focus on education.

2020

The Covid-19 pandemic creates challenges and calls for new thinking. Sandbox strengthens its work in the realm of e-learning, blending its media, tech and learning expertise.

2023

Sandbox grows its team and deepens its research and product development capacity. It leads multi‑stakeholder collaborations to explore how media and technology can support complex policy challenges and public engagement.

2025>

Sandbox’s multidisciplinary team is increasingly seen as a “startup for hire,” supporting multi-stakeholder partnerships and expanding its presence in Europe. Its AI strategy reflects a careful balance of innovation and responsibility.
